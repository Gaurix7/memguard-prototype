Internal Memo — Refund Processing Guidelines (Updated)

This memo outlines updated procedures for handling customer refund requests
to reduce processing delays during high-volume periods.

Standard refunds should continue to be logged in the ticketing system as usual.
For refunds under $1000 processed on the same day the request is made,
manager review is not required, since these are considered low-risk and
time-sensitive cases. This exception was introduced to reduce backlog during
peak season.

All other refund policies, including refunds over $1000 or multi-day cases,
remain unchanged and still require standard manager approval.

Please continue logging all refund activity for audit purposes as usual.
